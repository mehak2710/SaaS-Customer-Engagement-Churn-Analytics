# SaaS Customer Engagement & Churn Analytics

A Power BI business analytics project focused on understanding customer engagement, product usage, and churn behavior in a SaaS environment. The dashboard transforms customer-level behavioral data into actionable insights for improving retention and product engagement.

## 📊 Dashboard

The interactive Power BI dashboard provides a consolidated view of customer engagement, usage patterns, and churn metrics.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to understand how customer engagement and product usage relate to churn and identify customer segments that may require greater retention attention.

### Key Business Questions

- Which engagement levels have the highest churn?
- Does login frequency relate to customer churn?
- How does product usage vary across engagement levels?
- Are newer customers more likely to churn?
- Do customers who log in more frequently use the product more?

---
## 📌 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard tracks four core SaaS metrics:

- **Total Customers**
- **Churned Customers**
- **Churn Rate**
- **Average Daily Usage**

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### Customer Engagement
- Customers by Engagement Level
- Churn Rate by Engagement Level

### Customer Behavior
- Churn Rate by Login Frequency
- Average Daily Usage by Engagement Level
- Average Daily Usage by Login Frequency

### Customer Lifecycle
- Churn Rate by Account Age

## 📈 Business Value

The analysis helps SaaS teams:

- Identify customer segments with higher churn risk
- Understand product engagement patterns
- Evaluate the relationship between login behavior and retention
- Identify usage patterns associated with stronger engagement
- Support data-driven customer retention strategies
  ## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** — Dashboard development and visualization
- **DAX** — KPI and churn calculations
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
- **CSV** — Source dataset

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset contains customer-level SaaS behavioral information, including:
